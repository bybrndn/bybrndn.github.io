SATIONS

Type Videography & Editing
Year 2023
By Branden Hall, Varish Nair, Denzel Pereiea
Tools Premiere Pro, After Effects

A music video shot and edited through Auckland City at night — handheld, grainy, and vintage. A dreamy old-school aesthetic brought to life through filmmaking and After Effects.

Overview

My friends wanted me to shoot and edit their music video. They gave me some inspiration and a theme they were going for. Although informal, this project showcases my videography and editing skills in a real creative context.

The theme they wanted was a dreamy, vintage aesthetic — handheld footage with a grainy filter. That "Old Skool" vibe of a music video. We took to the streets of Auckland City at night and captured the scenes as we roamed.

Filming

I independently filmed each scene for this music video. We knew we wanted it to take place within the city — Auckland at night gave us the atmosphere we needed. Most scenes were improvised on the spot as we moved through the streets.

Editing & VFX

Several After Effects VFX shots were used throughout the video — including a VHS intro and outro effect with cinemascope bars, and a climax sequence where camera motion tracks between the two artists going bar for bar. This was the one planned scene in the whole video.

Reflection

Since this was one of my first proper uses of After Effects and videography skills together, looking back I feel like I could've spent a bit more time on the edit and pushed the VFX further. My skills have grown a lot since 2023 — but this project was a solid foundation and a real-world test of what I'd been learning.
