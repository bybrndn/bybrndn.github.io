A full visual identity system built for JAGZ — an IRL streamer from New Zealand making his presence known on the streets and on screen.

Overview

JAGZ is an IRL streamer from New Zealand — taking to the streets and building a community one stream at a time. This visual identity was built to match that energy and give him a look that stands out. Starting from the original JAGZ logo, the identity was expanded into a full system — refining the colour palette, establishing logo variations, and building out a consistent set of visual rules that flex across streaming and social.

Neon-shifted red and crisp white cut through deep navy, capturing the feel of city lights after dark. Bold, electric, and built to last. The result is a visual language that puts JAGZ on the map — loud enough to stop the scroll, considered enough to grow with him.
