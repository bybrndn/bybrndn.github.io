Overview

Move Media is an Auckland-based boutique video production house specialising in event films, corporate videos, social media content, videography, photography, and post-production. With over 20 years in the industry, they create beautiful, meaningful visuals that capture moments and tell compelling stories.

The brief was to build a brand identity that reflected their vision — to be Aotearoa's trusted creative partner for storytelling videos that emotionally move people and elevate brands worldwide. The result is a clean, confident system built around the values of storytelling, excellence, true collaboration, and real impact.

Logomark

The logomark is a minimalist "M" formed by three geometric elements — a small upward-angled triangle symbolising picking yourself up, a gentle angled line for smooth transition and flow, and a tall solid vertical beam representing stability, strength, and forward momentum.
