Overview

Te Whāriki Manawāhine o Hauraki represents a visual identity system developed to celebrate and preserve traditional Māori women's patterns from the Hauraki region. This project explores the intersection between cultural heritage and contemporary design, creating a visual system that honors tradition while being applicable to modern contexts.

The visual system draws inspiration from traditional weaving patterns (whāriki), natural forms of the Hauraki landscape, and significant cultural symbols to create an identity deeply rooted in the past while looking toward the future.

Typography System

The typography system combines two complementary typefaces: Cormorant Garamond for display and heading text, and DM Sans for body text and labels. This pairing creates a balance between classical elegance and contemporary readability.
