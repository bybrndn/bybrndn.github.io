FLICT AR

Type Branding, UI/UX, AR Game Design
Year 2024
By Branden Hall & Kavish Kuver
Tools Figma, Photoshop, Unity

An augmented reality mobile game built to feel physical and active — not something you play in isolation. Bold branding, AR gameplay, and a full UI/UX system designed in Figma.

Overview

During university, a friend and I began developing an augmented reality mobile game called CONFLICT. We set out to make a game that feels physical and active — not something you play in isolation. The visual direction needed to feel bold, energetic, and fresh.

AR is familiar tech, but we wanted to use it in a way that feels more purposeful and engaging. The brand uses sharp, drop-shadowed squares as its main visual element — representing the tile system in the game and tying the branding directly back to core gameplay.

UI / UX

Designing for augmented reality meant thinking carefully about what lives on screen versus in the real world. Previous experiments with AR and app development gave me a foundation to iterate from — studying what worked on a small mobile screen and what pulled focus away from the gameplay.
